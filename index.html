Zoltán Majnek
Software Engineer

Programming became Zoltán’s passion at a very young age. He explored every single bit of his first PC, the almost 1 MHz C64 supercomputer, and he wrote small demos in assembly language even before attending a computer technology secondary school. He still remembers some C64 machine code (not just ASM mnemonics!) to this day.

He graduated as a computer programmer in the last millennium. After a short break (2.5 decades of IT work), he enrolled at Óbudai University in the fall of 2022, where he recently earned his BSc degree in Computer Science Engineering with distinction, specializing in Big Data and Business Intelligence.

Although he is attracted to lower-level languages (ASM, Pascal, C++), he mainly deals with C# these days. He has gained extensive experience with .NET and ASP.NET, and has a strong background in Business Intelligence (building SQL/NoSQL to SSIS and Power BI workflows) alongside Apache Spark.

Space simulation games and strategy games are on the list of his hobbies, and working out also took up a lot of his free time.
